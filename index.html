Who We Are

A Precision Medicine Cardio­Immunology Group

Based at the Dept. of Cardiac, Thoracic, Vascular Sciences and Public Health at Univ. Padova, we combine clinical expertise, advanced imaging, molecular biology, and data science to push the boundaries of inflammatory heart disease management.

Translational Research

From bench to bedside: autoantibody profiling, viral genomics, and immune pathway analysis informing clinical practice.

Global Collaboration Network

Active partnerships with ESC working groups, European registries, and research institutions across 3 continents.

Education & Training

Directors of CARDIM — Italy's only dedicated postgraduate master in Cardiac Immunology at Univ. Padova.

Research Areas

Our Scientific Focus

Six core research domains shaping our understanding of inflammatory heart disease and immune-cardiac crosstalk.

01

Myocarditis Diagnosis & Prognosis

Clinical predictors of relapse, death, or transplant. EMB-guided classification of viral vs. autoimmune forms for targeted therapy.

EMBBiomarkersRegistry
02

Cardiac MRI & Multi-Modality Imaging

CMR tissue characterisation, T1/T2 mapping, late gadolinium enhancement, and novel sequences for inflammation quantification.

CMRT1/T2 MappingLGE
03

Autoimmune Cardiomyopathy

Cardiac autoantibody profiling, genetic-environmental factors, HLA associations, and immunosuppressive treatment optimisation.

AutoantibodiesHLAImmunosuppression
04

Pericardial Disease & Pericarditis

Recurrent pericarditis: immunological mechanisms, IL-1 pathway targeting, colchicine and IL-1 blockers in clinical practice.

ColchicineIL-1Recurrence
05

Machine Learning & Computational Cardiology

AI-driven outcome models integrating clinical, imaging, and omics data for personalised risk prediction and therapeutic decision support.

Deep LearningOmicsPrediction
06

Systemic Immune Disease & the Heart

Cardiac manifestations in systemic lupus, sarcoidosis, giant cell arteritis, and other autoimmune conditions. Interdisciplinary management.

LupusSarcoidosisGCA

Education

CARDIM: Italy's Only Postgraduate Master in Cardiac Immunology

A Level II Master's programme at the University of Padova (Unipd Executive Learning), delivering the advanced clinical and scientific knowledge needed to manage patients with myocarditis, pericarditis, and other immune-mediated inflammatory heart diseases.

Who should apply?

Cardiologists, rheumatologists, internists, and physicians seeking deep expertise in cardiac inflammation and immune-mediated conditions.

Open to international participants

The programme welcomes applications from physicians across Europe and internationally. Lectures delivered by leading European experts.
