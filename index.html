Who We Are

A Precision Medicine Cardio-Immunology Hub

Based at the Department of Cardiac, Thoracic, Vascular Sciences and Public Health at the University of Padova, we combine clinical cardiology, advanced imaging, molecular biology, and biostatistics to set new standards in inflammatory heart disease.

Translational Research

Autoantibody profiling, viral genomics, and immune pathway analysis directly informing clinical immunosuppressive therapy.

Global Collaboration Network

Active coordination with ESC Working Groups, international registries, and academic institutions across Europe and the US.

Postgraduate Training

Founders of CARDIM — Italy's landmark postgraduate Master in Cardioimmunologia for cardiologists and internists worldwide.

Scientific Focus

Core Research Areas

Six interlinked scientific domains advancing precision diagnosis and personalized care in inflammatory cardiac conditions.

01

Myocarditis Diagnosis & Prognosis

Identifying clinical, biomarker, and histological predictors of relapse, heart failure, and transplantation in acute and chronic myocarditis.

EMBBiomarkersPrognosis
02

Cardiac MRI & Multimodality Imaging

Quantitative tissue characterization using T1/T2 mapping, extracellular volume (ECV), and LGE for non-invasive inflammation monitoring.

CMR MappingLGEEACVI
03

Autoimmune Cardiomyopathy

Profiling anti-heart and anti-intercalated disk autoantibodies (AHA/AIDA) and assessing immunosuppressive treatment efficacy.

AutoantibodiesHLAImmunomodulation
04

Pericardial Disease & Recurrent Pericarditis

Dissecting autoinflammatory mechanisms, IL-1 pathway targeting, and clinical trials with colchicine and interleukin-1 blockers.

PericarditisIL-1 BlockersColchicine
05

Machine Learning & AI Cardiology

Developing computational prediction models combining clinical variables, imaging parameters, and high-dimensional omics profiles.

Machine LearningBiostatisticsOmics
06

Systemic Immune Diseases & Heart

Managing cardiac manifestations in systemic lupus erythematosus, sarcoidosis, vasculitis, and immune-checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) myocarditis.

SarcoidosisLupusICI Myocarditis

Postgraduate Education

CARDIM: Master in Cardioimmunologia

The University of Padova offers Italy's first and only Level II Postgraduate Master in Cardioimmunology (Unipd Executive Learning), equipping cardiologists, rheumatologists, and clinical specialists worldwide with state-of-the-art diagnostic and therapeutic tools.

Target Audience

Cardiologists, rheumatologists, internists, and emergency physicians seeking specialized mastery in inflammatory heart conditions.

International Faculty

Lectures and workshops delivered by leading European and global faculty members from the ESC and major university hospitals.
