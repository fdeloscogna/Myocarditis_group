About the Group

Clinical & Translational Cardioimmunology

Based at the Department of Cardiac, Thoracic, Vascular Sciences and Public Health, University of Padova, the group integrates clinical cardiology, advanced cardiac MRI, molecular immunology, and biostatistics.

Translational Immunology

Serum autoantibodies, systemic inflammatory markers, and lymphocyte subsets guiding tailored medical treatments.

International Collaborations & Registries

Active coordination with the ESC Working Group on Myocardial and Pericardial Diseases, multicenter clinical registries, and international academic hospitals.

Medical Education

Academic leadership of CARDIM, the postgraduate Level II Master course in Cardioimmunology at the University of Padova.

Research Areas

Scientific Focus Areas

Primary research domains in myocarditis, pericarditis, and inflammatory heart diseases.

01

Myocarditis Diagnosis & Long Term Prognosis

Evaluation of clinical, biomarker, and histological determinants of relapse, heart failure progression, and transplantation in acute and chronic myocarditis.

EMBBiomarkersPrognosis
02

Cardiac MRI & Multimodality Imaging

Tissue characterization using parametric T1 and T2 mapping, extracellular volume fraction, and late gadolinium enhancement for myocardial and pericardial inflammation.

CMR MappingPericardial LGEEACVI
03

Autoimmune Cardiomyopathy

Characterization of anti heart and anti intercalated disk autoantibodies (AHA, AIDA) and assessment of immunosuppressive therapy efficacy in biopsy proven cases.

AutoantibodiesHLAImmunomodulation
04

Pericardial Disease & Recurrent Pericarditis

Investigation of autoinflammatory pathways, interleukin 1 signaling, recurrence predictors, colchicine resistance, and targeted clinical response to anti IL 1 agents (anakinra, rilonacept).

Recurrent PericarditisIL 1 BlockersColchicineAnakinra
05

Biostatistics & Predictive Modeling

Application of machine learning and statistical models combining clinical registry data, imaging metrics, and immunological profiles for personalized outcome prediction.

Machine LearningBiostatisticsComputational Cardiology
06

Systemic Immune Diseases & Cardiac Involvement

Clinical management and investigation of cardiac manifestations in systemic lupus erythematosus, sarcoidosis, vasculitis, and immune checkpoint inhibitor myocarditis.

SarcoidosisLupusICI Myocarditis

Prof. Alida L.P. Caforio
Group Lead, Principal Investigator
Associate Professor of Cardiology, University of Padova
Chair, ESC Long term Cardiomyopathy and Myocarditis Registry

Cardiologist and academic researcher with over thirty years of clinical and laboratory work on the pathogenesis, diagnosis, and treatment of myocarditis, pericarditis, and inflammatory cardiomyopathies. Director of the CARDIM Master programme and coordinator of ESC guidelines and task forces on myocardial and pericardial diseases. Investigator in cardiac autoantibodies, targeted immunomodulation, and endomyocardial biopsy guided therapies.

Dr. Anna Baritussio
Cardiovascular Imaging Expert
Consultant Cardiologist, Padova University Hospital
Former EACVI CMR Councillor, Level 3 EACVI Certified

Cardiologist specialized in cardiovascular magnetic resonance with advanced fellowship training at the Bristol Heart Institute. Certified EACVI and SCMR Level 3 expert. Research focuses on parametric mapping techniques (T1, T2), extracellular volume quantification, and late gadolinium enhancement for myocarditis, pericardial inflammation, and constrictive pericarditis.

Postgraduate Education

CARDIM: Master in Cardioimmunology

Level II Postgraduate Master programme organized by the University of Padova (Unipd Executive Learning), offering specialized clinical and diagnostic curriculum in myocarditis, acute and recurrent pericarditis, and inflammatory heart diseases for physicians worldwide.

Participant Profile

Cardiologists, rheumatologists, internists, and clinical specialists seeking advanced training in myocardial and pericardial inflammation.

Faculty & Lecturers
