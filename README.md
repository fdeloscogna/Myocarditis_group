# CardioImmunology & Myocarditis Research Group — University of Padova

Official presentation and networking website for the **CardioImmunology & Myocarditis Research Group** at the Department of Cardiac, Thoracic, Vascular Sciences and Public Health, University of Padova, Italy.

## 🫀 About the Group
We are a multidisciplinary team dedicated to understanding, diagnosing, and treating myocarditis, pericarditis, and immune-mediated inflammatory heart diseases.
